SUDOKU APK - STEP BY STEP (about 15 minutes, nothing to install)

A) Put the project on GitHub
1. Go to github.com and create a free account (or log in).
2. Click the "+" at the top right, then "New repository".
   Name it: sudoku    Keep it Private    Click "Create repository".
3. On the next page click the link "uploading an existing file".
4. Unzip this project on your PC. Open the unzipped folder, select EVERYTHING
   inside it (the .github folder, www folder, and the files) and drag it into
   the GitHub page. Wait until all files are listed, then click "Commit changes".
   Check that the repo shows: .github, www, package.json, capacitor.config.json.
   (If ".github" is missing: click "Add file" > "Create new file", type
   .github/workflows/build.yml as the name and paste the contents of that file.)

B) Let GitHub build the APK
5. Click the "Actions" tab. If asked, click the green button to enable workflows.
6. Open "Build Android APK" and wait about 5-8 minutes for a green check mark.
   (If nothing started: click "Run workflow" on the right.)
7. Click the finished run, scroll to "Artifacts" at the bottom and click
   "sudoku-apk" to download a zip. Unzip it: inside is app-debug.apk

C) Install on your Samsung phone
8. Send app-debug.apk to your phone (USB cable, Google Drive, or email to yourself).
9. Open it from "My Files". If asked, allow installing from this source.
   If Play Protect warns, choose "Install anyway" (the app is your own).
10. Open "Sudoku" - it works fully offline.

NOTES
- Your saved game and statistics stay on the phone. They are deleted only if
  you uninstall the app (updating over the old APK keeps them).
- To update the game later: replace www/index.html in GitHub (open it, click the
  pencil, or upload the new file with the same name) and repeat steps 6-9.
